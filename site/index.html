Québec French

A free web app for learning the French actually spoken in Québec. It works in any browser, installs on a phone like an app, and needs no account.

Open Québec French

What it does

  • Dictionary: look up French words in any form, with Québec pronunciation (IPA) and English meanings.
  • Flashcards: spaced-repetition vocabulary cards, new words starting at CEFR level A1.
  • Lessons: pronunciation, grammar and everyday themes, with Québec usage throughout.
  • Audio: an optional pack of spoken clips for every word and example.
  • Achievements: study time, words learned and your CEFR level.

Optional Google sign-in

Everything works without signing in; progress stays in your browser. If you choose “Sign in with Google” on the Achievements page, the app saves your card progress and study time to a private, hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so your devices can add them up. It uses the drive.appdata permission, which reaches only that folder, and your email address, shown so you know which account syncs. There is no server of ours; nothing is sent to the developer. Details are in the privacy policy.
