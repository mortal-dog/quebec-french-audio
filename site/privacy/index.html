Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy covers Québec French, a web app at quebec-french.doctornova.net that can be installed on a phone. The same text is in the app at Settings → Privacy policy.

The short version

Québec French collects nothing. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server operated by the developer. Review progress and study time stay in your browser on this device, unless you export them yourself or turn on the optional Google Drive sync, which copies them to a private folder in your own Google Drive and nowhere else.

What stays on the device

Flashcard progress, study time (minutes per day, counted while the app is open and in use), the chosen voice and the downloaded audio are stored in this browser's storage for the site, on this device only. Clearing the site's data in the browser (or uninstalling the installed app) deletes them. The optional “Export progress” feature downloads a file that you control; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Optional sign-in with Google and Drive sync

The Achievements page offers “Sign in with Google” to sync between your own devices. It is off until you tap it. When you sign in:

  • Google asks you to let the app use its own app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission) and to see your email address, which the page shows so you know which account is syncing. The app folder is hidden from your Drive view, and this permission does not let the app see, open or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Each device writes one file to that folder: its card progress, its study minutes per day, a random device id and a label such as “iPhone · Safari”. Each device reads the other devices' files to add them up.
  • The browser talks to Google directly (accounts.google.com, www.googleapis.com). The developer has no server, never receives the files or your email, and cannot see them.
  • The sign-in token is kept in this browser's storage for up to an hour. Signing out revokes it with Google. The files stay in your Drive until you delete them: in Google Drive's settings, under “Manage apps”, choose Québec French and “Delete hidden app data”. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google's privacy policy governs your Google account and Drive.

Québec French's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to sync your own progress between your own devices; it is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to anyone, and not read by any person.

Network requests

The app is a web page served by GitHub Pages: opening it fetches the page from GitHub's servers, and after the first visit the browser keeps a copy so that it also opens offline. Downloading the optional pronunciation audio fetches files from french.doctornova.net, also served by GitHub Pages, and opening Settings asks that host, without downloading the audio, how large the download is. As with any web request, GitHub's servers see your IP address and standard request logs; the app sends nothing else with these requests. GitHub's own privacy statement governs their logs. Apart from the optional Google sync above, the app makes no other network requests of its own.

Two kinds of link open in your browser, and only when you tap them: a daily card's search link (Google Search, with the card's topic) and a dictionary entry's Translate link (Google Translate, with that word). Google's privacy policy governs those pages; the app sends nothing else.

Data collection, sharing, and sale

None. The developer collects no personal data, shares none, and sells none. What the optional sync writes goes to your own Google Drive only. There is no third-party SDK for analytics, advertising, or crash reporting; Google's sign-in script is loaded only when you tap “Sign in with Google” or “Sync now”.

Children

The app has no chat, no user-generated content, no purchases, and the developer collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this policy and the in-app copy will be updated before that version ships, and the “Last updated” date will change.

Contact
